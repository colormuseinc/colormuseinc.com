How to Choose Colours in the Arc'teryx Sale

So many colourful Arc'teryx pieces are on sale. How do you pick the colours that suit you? Today we sorted them by how versatile each colour is, so almost everyone can find colourful outdoor gear that works for them.

This time we did not sort Arc'teryx season by season. We sorted it by how versatile each colour is.

A lot of people get stuck when shopping: "Is this really my season?" In fact, only a few very special colours need to match your exact season. Most colours work across neighbouring seasons. Light Summer and Light Winter, Light Spring and Light Summer, Soft Spring and Soft Autumn all have a lot in common.

When you shop, go by the four qualities of your colours: light, bright, soft or deep. If a piece looks at least 80% right on you, it is a good buy.

A note on colour: some official photos look a little different from the pieces in real life. We sorted everything by the real-life colour. Some colours look lighter in person, and some look a bit deeper.

Below are three groups: warm colours, shoes for the Light types, and cool colours. Each piece shows the season it suits best. Tap any piece to see it on arcteryx.com.

Warm colours · Spring & Autumn

Colours with a warm, golden undertone. Mostly Light Spring and Bright Spring here: soft pink, fresh green and candy pink. The yellow Sidewinder is Bright Autumn.

始祖鸟打折单品，彩色系该怎么选？

始祖鸟打折单品彩色好多，怎么选择适合自己的彩色呢？今天给大家做了一个比较通用性的整理，基本上所有宝宝都可以找到适合自己的彩色户外运动服哦。

这次的始祖鸟，我们没有按 12 季型一型一型分，而是按「通用性」来整理。

很多人购物时会卡住：这件到底是不是我的季型？其实只有一些非常特殊的颜色，才需要完全对准自己的季型去选。大多数颜色，几个季型都能通用：比如浅夏和浅冬、浅春和浅夏、柔春和柔秋，都有共通点。

日常购物时，抓住自己适合颜色的四个元素：浅、亮、柔、深，对号入座就好。一件衣服上身只要 80% 以上好看，就可以买。

另外，部分衣服官网照片和实物有一点色差。我们是结合实物的颜色来帮大家分类的：有的颜色实物看上去会更浅一点，有的会更深一点。

下面分三组：春秋暖色、浅型都好搭的鞋、夏冬冷色。每件都标了最适合的季型，点卡片直接进始祖鸟官网。

暖色 · 春 & 秋

带暖调、偏金黄底色的颜色。这一组以浅春、亮春为主：浅粉、嫩芽绿、糖果粉；黄色那件 Sidewinder 是亮秋。
