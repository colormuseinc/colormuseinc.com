Not sure which colours suit you? This guide covers all 12 colour seasons at Aritzia.

Light Spring

Your colours: soft and sunny: peach, butter yellow, coral pink, mint and sky blue.

How to choose:
1. Light, not bright. Look for pastels with a warm, creamy base.
2. Skip black and stark white. Ivory and cream are your neutrals.
3. Keep contrast low. Pair pastels with pastels instead of dark colours.
4. Makeup: peach, apricot and warm pink. Avoid deep berry and cool mauve.

Soft Spring

Your colours: warm but gentle: pistachio, butter yellow, peach, dusty rose and sand.

How to choose:
1. Warm, but never neon. Choose colours with a soft, creamy feel.
2. Your white is a warm ivory, especially in textured or chunky knits.
3. Go tone-on-tone: pistachio with butter yellow, peach with sand.
4. Makeup: peach, warm rosy beige and soft coral. Avoid icy pinks and very dark lips.

Bright Spring

Your colours: clear and vivid: tomato red, apricot orange, sunny yellow, coral, grass green and bright blue.

How to choose:
1. Clear and warm. Your colours should look fresh and juicy, never dusty.
2. You can wear real brights near your face. Greyish, muted shades make you look tired.
3. Let one bright colour lead and keep the rest light and clean.
4. Makeup: coral, warm red and peachy pink with a glow. Avoid muted browns and cool berry.

Light Summer

Soft Autumn

4. Makeup: rosewood, terracotta and soft coral. Avoid icy pinks and fuchsia.

浅春 Light Spring

适合的颜色：蜜桃、奶黄、浅珊瑚粉、奶白。

怎么选：
1. 要浅不要艳：选带暖调、像奶油一样的浅色。
2. 避开黑色和正白，象牙白、奶油白是你的基础色。
3. 低对比：浅色配浅色，不要配深色。
4. 彩妆：蜜桃、杏色、暖粉；避开深莓色和冷调豆沙紫。
